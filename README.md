## 📖 About the Movie

**Transformers** is a **science fiction action film** released in **2007**, directed by **Michael Bay** and produced by **Steven Spielberg**.  
It’s based on the popular **Hasbro toy line**, where two robotic alien factions — the **Autobots** and the **Decepticons** — bring their intergalactic war to Earth.
